Kitout landing page concepts

Three distinct desktop directions for a declarative Mac setup tool: infrastructure trust, native Mac polish, and an operational workbench.

Recommendation: Start with Trusted Infrastructure, then borrow the Apple-native finish from concept 2. It feels safe enough for a developer to let Kitout inspect and change their machine.
